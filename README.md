# H.A.N.K. — Browser Text Adventure

An interactive text adventure game set in the year 2735. You control **Hank** (Humanoid Artificial iNtelligence K-215), a robot dispatched from the starship *The Collective* to investigate an alien planet. After a rough landing, Hank must survive, navigate an unfamiliar world, and find his way back to the ship — guided by your decisions.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No install, no server, no dependencies.

## Gameplay

- **Choice-driven narrative** — your decisions shape the story and determine how it ends
- **Resource management** — keep an eye on Hank's battery and physical condition; both are limited and many actions have a cost
- **Exploration** — discover items, learn new abilities, and unlock paths you might otherwise miss
- **Consequences** — choices matter; how you treat others and what you choose to do will be remembered
- **Checkpoints** — the game auto-saves at key moments; death lets you restart or reload from the last checkpoint
- **Multiple endings** — play through more than once to see how different choices lead to different outcomes

## Built With

- [Twine 2.6.1](https://twinery.org/) — interactive fiction editor
- [Harlowe 3.3.4](https://twine2.neocities.org/) — Twine story format
- Single self-contained HTML file (~630 KB)
